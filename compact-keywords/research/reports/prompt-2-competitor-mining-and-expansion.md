## Incremental findings

A direct competitor, `virtuvesjums.lv`, demonstrates current visibility not only for broad custom-kitchen terms but also for built-in, manufacture, price and configuration themes. Particularly useful overlap signals include `iebūvējamās virtuves cenas`, `virtuves iekārtas izgatavošana`, `virtuves mēbeļu izgatavošana` and `virtuves sala`. These are evidence for later SERP comparison, not final page decisions.

The competitor also ranks through broad or imperfectly matched pages for several themes. This supports testing whether an existing mapped page could be improved before proposing another page. The main cannibalization groups for Prompt 3 are:

- homepage/custom-kitchen vs custom-unit variants;
- design vs planning vs interior language;
- manufacture vs custom-unit language;
- built-in vs custom;
- price vs installation-price;
- component/retail vs complete-project intent.

## Local evidence

The 30 tested combinations covering all 20 confirmed locations plus a small manufacture/custom-unit sample returned no Keyword Overview rows. Values remain null rather than being converted to zero. Local pages should not be assumed to have measurable demand; only a limited live-SERP sample should proceed to Prompt 3.

## Business-boundary handling

Complete tailored kitchen-project terms were retained even where volume was low, zero, or unavailable. Retail kitchen sets, standalone cabinets, facades, fittings, worktops, appliances, tables, accessories, professional kitchens and unrelated lexical matches were retained separately in the rejected table with reasons.

Installation-only intent remains uncertain. Phrases such as `virtuves uzstādīšanas cena` are shortlisted only for SERP and service-boundary validation, not treated as a verified standalone service.

## Competitor separation

Direct custom-kitchen providers from Prompt 1 remain separate from adjacent providers, marketplaces, publishers and retailers. Retail-heavy component terms are not used as proof of demand for the verified complete custom-kitchen service.
