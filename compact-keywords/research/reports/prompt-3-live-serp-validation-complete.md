## Main factual patterns

### Core custom-kitchen phrases

The singular, plural, “uz pasūtījumu,” kitchen-furniture and kitchen-unit variants all return substantially the same visitor need: a provider that designs and produces a tailored kitchen. Direct custom-kitchen providers dominate, with occasional marketplace, article and video results. Singular/plural wording does not produce distinct intent.

### Manufacture, built-in and complete-project phrases

Manufacturing phrases remain commercially relevant but are more mixed than the core custom-kitchen phrase. Retail categories, marketplaces, price pages and calculators enter the top results. “Virtuves izgatavošana un uzstādīšana” aligns more directly with the verified complete-project offer than installation-only wording.

### Design, project and planning

“Virtuves dizains” is strongly informational and inspiration-led. “Virtuves projektēšana” has materially stronger service intent, including dedicated project/planning pages. “Virtuves plānošana” and “funkcionāls virtuves plānojums” are predominantly informational.

### Price, estimate and installation

“Virtuves cenas aprēķins” and “iebūvējamās virtuves cenas” are led by calculators, price pages and commercial guides. “Virtuves tāme” is ambiguous and includes renovation-budget and estimating-service results. “Virtuves uzstādīšanas cena” is installation-only/trade intent and falls outside the verified complete-project boundary.

### Cabinets, facades, fittings and retail contamination

The validated shortlist did not retain separate facade or fittings terms, but the component-adjacent SERPs represented by “stūra virtuves,” “virtuves sala,” built-in-unit terms and installation pricing show substantial retailer, marketplace and ecommerce presence. The noun-only “virtuves sala” query is overwhelmingly product-led.

### Configuration variants

- “Stūra virtuves” is heavily retail/product-led.
- “L veida virtuve” and “U veida virtuve” are mixed informational, project and retail SERPs.
- “Virtuve ar salu” is mixed and includes custom projects; “virtuves sala” is a different, ecommerce-heavy visitor need.
- “Virtuve ar pussalu” is sparse and mostly informational/social.
- “Vienas sienas virtuve” is semantically ambiguous because Google returns wall-panel content.
- “Paralēla virtuve” is noisy and weakly resolved.
- “Gaiteņa tipa virtuve” is mostly informational.

### Residential situations

“Virtuve privātmājai” returns project galleries and examples. “Virtuve jaunbūvei” returns many direct providers but most do not target the modifier explicitly. “Virtuve mazam dzīvoklim” and the Hruščov-era phrase are mainly informational/project-led. “Atvērta plānojuma virtuve” is planning-led and informational.

### Local query

“Virtuves pēc pasūtījuma Rīga” returns a local pack and direct providers, but its organic results substantially reuse the core non-local competitors. This validates local relevance for Riga but does not independently prove a distinct city-page need.

## Lower-authority and nontraditional results

Smaller local providers frequently rank on core, manufacturing, built-in and residential-project queries. Social posts, project galleries and small specialist sites also rank for configuration and residential-situation phrases. This indicates that high domain authority is not an absolute prerequisite where a page directly demonstrates the relevant kitchen need. Authority/backlink metrics were not returned by the SERP endpoint used in this run, so no authority values were estimated.

## Existing-page awareness

The brief records an existing homepage and mapped service pages. The current domain is not indexed, so the live SERPs cannot demonstrate which existing URL Google would select. The evidence does show that core custom-kitchen phrases share one service need; design/planning phrases split between informational and service intent; manufacture/built-in phrases overlap but have retail contamination; and installation-only wording is distinct from the verified complete-project offer. No final existing-page-versus-new-page decision is made here.

## Metric omissions

Search volume, monthly trend, CPC, detected intent and organic difficulty were preserved from the available DataForSEO metric rows in the prior normalized shortlist. Blank values mean no metric row was returned or the metric was unavailable. No value was estimated. Paid competition is not a column in the required normalized schema and is therefore not added.

The package therefore includes the complete normalized response evidence used and a machine-readable limitation record; it does not mislabel reconstructed extracts as unmodified provider response bodies.
