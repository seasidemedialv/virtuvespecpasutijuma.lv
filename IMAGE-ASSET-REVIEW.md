# Real-project image review

Review date: 2026-07-31

Source: owner-supplied archive containing 53 JPEG photographs and one MP4 video. The owner identified the archive as real jobs completed by the business and requested selective website use.

## Editorial decision

The site should not use every file. A smaller, deliberately varied gallery provides stronger proof, avoids repetitive angles, and keeps unfinished work, unrelated rooms, personal details and weak compositions out of the public presentation.

## Publication safeguards

- Captions describe only clearly visible layout characteristics.
- No client names, addresses, project locations, dates, brands, materials, prices or technical specifications are inferred from photographs.
- Exact project details may be added later only from verified business records and with appropriate publication permission.
- The owner-supplied status is recorded as the authorization basis for this preview; production publication permission remains an owner/legal responsibility before launch.
