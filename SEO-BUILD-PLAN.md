# Measured-Demand SEO Build Plan

**Project:** virtuvespecpasutijuma.lv  
**Research source:** `virtuvespecpasutijuma-full-research-2026-07-28`  
**Status:** Owner-directed required build scope  
**Rule:** An SEO landing page appears in this plan only when the completed DataForSEO dataset contains measurable demand for a relevant Latvian keyword. Functional/legal pages are tracked separately and are not presented as keyword-demand pages.

## Interpretation rules

- Volumes below are DataForSEO monthly estimates from 2026-07-28, not guaranteed traffic.
- Similar keyword volumes must not be added together because variants may represent overlapping demand.
- A measurable keyword does not permit unsupported business claims.
- Each page must have its own primary intent, content structure, examples and internal-link role.
- Closely related pages must link to one canonical parent topic and must not repeat the same copy.
- Irrelevant measured keywords, competitor brands, retailer searches, products and services the business does not provide remain excluded.

## Required page-separation rules

To pursue broad coverage without creating duplicate pages:

- Homepage: complete offer, proof, major paths and conversion.
- Custom kitchen units: what constitutes the complete made-to-measure unit.
- Manufacturing: production methods, quality control and deliverables.
- Built-in kitchens: integration with room dimensions and fixed constraints.
- Design: aesthetic/technical project development.
- Planning: layout, dimensions, work zones and ergonomics.
- Installation: site preparation, installation scope and handover.
- Prices: verified examples and cost factors only.
- Layout/style/component pages: answer their named problem using unique examples and specifications.

Every required page must receive a unique keyword assignment, title, H1, outline, FAQ set and internal-link role before copywriting begins.

## Excluded from this measured-demand plan

No standalone SEO page is approved here for:

- General interior design
- Air-conditioning, electrical, plumbing or unrelated installation services
- Ready-made kitchen sets or cheap modular retail kitchens
- Chairs, tables, appliances, mixers, extractor fans, rugs or accessories
- Competitor, retailer or review searches
- Unrelated manufacturing services
- U-shaped kitchens, classical kitchens, private-house kitchens or apartment-series pages because those topics were not measured in the completed dataset
- Worktop and material landing pages where the completed dataset did not establish a sufficiently specific kitchen query

## Location pages

The narrow follow-up measured 40 phrases across the 20 verified service areas. All returned no reportable volume. Consequently, location pages are not part of this **measurable-demand** build list.

The 20 areas remain verified business coverage and must be presented on the site. A location page may be added later when new measurable query evidence or sufficiently strong local project/search evidence supports it. No place-name-swapped pages are allowed.

## Functional pages tracked separately

These pages may still be required for usability, trust, conversion or legal compliance, but they are not represented as measurable-keyword landing pages:

- `/realizetie-projekti/`
- Individual verified project pages
- `/ka-mes-stradajam/`
- `/par-mums/`
- `/kontakti/`
- Privacy/cookie/legal pages

## Build order

1. Confirm the business scope and factual inputs required by each page.
2. Create a keyword-to-URL map and cannibalization boundaries for all 16 measured-demand pages.
3. Prepare unique briefs for all 16 pages.
4. Produce factual copy and real-evidence requirements page by page.
5. Design the shared system and page-specific modules.
6. Implement all required measured-demand pages plus necessary functional/legal pages.
7. Run content-overlap, claims, technical SEO, accessibility and responsive QA before indexation.

## Completion criterion

The measured-demand SEO scope is complete only when all 16 required URLs are implemented with materially distinct, factually supported content. A page must remain unpublished or `noindex` if the necessary business facts or unique supporting evidence are unavailable.
